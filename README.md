# Showdown

Rank your TV shows the way Beli ranks restaurants. Add a show, say whether you loved it, liked it, or it wasn't for you, then answer a few head-to-head comparisons. The show lands in the right spot and gets a 0 to 10 score from its position.

Everything is one HTML file. Rankings save in your browser; use Export backup to keep a copy or move it to another device.
